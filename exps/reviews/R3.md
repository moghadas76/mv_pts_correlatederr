**Q4. Graph construction without natural graph structure?**

Teger uses the graph only to rank edges by curvature and to form the Laplacian entering G_t; any sparse weighted graph supports both.

*Fully-connected will not work.* On a complete graph all edges are equivalent by symmetry, so curvature is constant and reweighting degenerates to a uniform multiplier — row (4) of our ablation, worse than no reweighting in 7/8. Curvature measures local bottleneck structure; a graph without bottlenecks offers it nothing. Sparsity is a requirement, not a convenience.

*kNN or thresholded-similarity graphs are the natural default*, from residual correlation, embedding distance, or any domain metric. Ours is of this form (Appendix J); nothing downstream uses road semantics.

*Learned graphs are a promising untested extension.* MTGNN already learns an adjacency, though Teger uses the provided graph even there. Feeding one into Algorithm 1 is natural, caveat that learned adjacencies are often dense, returning to the degeneracy above.

*Scope.* All four datasets have a physical road network, and our argument for reweighting over rewiring (Sec. 4.3) turns on edges being physically realisable, which has no analogue elsewhere. We have no experiments outside this regime and say so in the limitations.



Not by simply reversing the highlighted inequality. The highlighted result is a **lower bound on the worst-case energy gap**:

[
\max_{\sigma=\pm1}
\left{
F_\sigma!\left(x_\sigma^{(K)}\right)-F_{\sigma,\star}
\right}
\ge
\frac{a^2}{2}(D-2K-2)_+ .
]

Changing (\ge) to (\le) would be incorrect: an arbitrary local algorithm can produce an arbitrarily large error, so there is no universal upper bound of this form. The manuscript establishes the lower bound first and then obtains a matching upper bound on the **number of rounds** through SourceFlood. 

### Recommended upper-bound formulation

You can instead write the constructive result as

[
K_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{loc}}(m,a)
\le
\left\lfloor\frac{D-1}{2}\right\rfloor,
]

because SourceFlood returns an exact minimizer within that many rounds. Since the lower bound matches it for (0\le\varepsilon<a^2/2), the strongest formulation is the equality

[
K_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{loc}}(m,a)
===================================

\left\lfloor\frac{D-1}{2}\right\rfloor,
\qquad
0\le\varepsilon<\frac{a^2}{2}.
]

A suitable abstract revision would be:

> On paths, every (K)-local algorithm has worst-case energy gap at least (\frac{a^2}{2}(D-2K-2)_+). Conversely, SourceFlood achieves zero gap in at most (\lfloor(D-1)/2\rfloor) rounds, yielding the exact minimax local-round complexity (\lfloor(D-1)/2\rfloor) for every (0\le\varepsilon<a^2/2).

This clearly presents the first result as the lower bound and the constructive algorithm as the matching upper bound.
